Day One
Wheels Down, Adventure Up
Thursday, July 9
1:10 PM
✈ Arrival
Land at SLC, Grab the Keys
Touch down at Salt Lake City International. Pick up your rental car and take a breath — the red rock kingdom awaits 4 hours south. You've got a legendary pizza lunch on the agenda first, so no rushing.
2:00 PM
🍕 Lunch
Slackwater Pizzeria
No Reservation Needed
📋 View Menu
Welcome to SLC — and welcome to one of its most beloved spots. Slackwater is a locally-owned artisan pizza and craft beer institution that's been slinging incredible pies since 2011, voted Best Pizza and Best Beer Selection in Salt Lake City. The sourdough crust uses a touch of honey — light, slightly sweet, totally addictive. Their California Sunrise (green goddess dressing, roasted chicken, peppered bacon, tomatoes) is their bestseller for a reason. And with 300+ craft beers, the beer menu could keep you busy for a week. Luckily, you only have an afternoon.
💰 ~$10–20/person 🍺 300+ craft beers 🌿 Vegan & gluten-free options
👕 Attire

Come as you are — literally. This is a casual, come-as-you-land spot in the Granary District. Airport clothes, hiking gear, whatever you rolled off the plane in. The vibe is laid-back local. Save the fashion choices for somewhere that asks for them.

3:00 PM
🚗 Drive + Grocery Stop
WinCo Foods (Provo, UT) → Bryce Canyon (3.5 hrs)
Stock up on water (you will need more than you think — Utah is merciless), snacks, and anything you'll need for the week. Once you leave civilization heading south, grocery options become very sparse. Head down I-15, then east on Hwy 89 through stunning canyon country — the drive itself is a preview of what's to come.
🌡️ Pro Tip Bryce Canyon sits at over 8,000 feet elevation — dramatically cooler than the valley. That said, July afternoons can still be hot on exposed trails. Pack layers AND sunscreen. The Utah sun at altitude doesn't mess around.
7:30 PM
🌌 Check In
Clear Sky Resort — Your Home Base
Welcome to one of the most extraordinary places you will ever sleep. Clear Sky Resort is the first and only glass dome resort in the United States — a constellation of geodesic Sky Domes nestled in a private 80-acre canyon, 15 minutes from Bryce Canyon National Park. Floor-to-ceiling panoramic glass means you can literally stargaze from bed. The Bryce Canyon area is a designated International Dark Sky Park — on a clear night, the Milky Way is so vivid it looks painted. Luxury 5-star mattresses, rainfall showers, private patios with fire pits. This isn't camping. This is glamping at its absolute zenith.
🌟 International Dark Sky Park 🔭 15 min from Bryce Canyon NP 🏔️ Private 80-acre canyon
The night sky at Bryce Canyon is genuinely otherworldly. No filter needed — or possible.
8:15 PM
🍽️ Dinner
Showdowns Restaurant
No Reservation Needed
📋 View Menu
Classic Southern Utah roadhouse fare after a long travel day. Think hearty portions, honest cooking, no fuss. You've been in airports and cars — you've earned something comforting and no-nonsense.
👕 Attire

Casual Western. Jeans and a t-shirt is the universal uniform. Flip flops and hiking clothes are completely at home. Nobody's dressing up on night one.

Day Two
Full Bryce Canyon Day — Hoodoo Madness
Friday, July 10
5:00 AM
🌄 Morning Hike
Sunrise at Sunrise Point
Yes, this is a 5 AM alarm. No, you will not regret it. Sunrise Point sits at 8,100 feet at the northernmost edge of the famous Bryce Amphitheater. As the sun clears the horizon, thousands of pink-orange hoodoos below you literally glow like embers. This is one of the most photographed sunrises in America for a reason. The trail is an easy paved 0.4-mile walk, so you can spend your energy jaw-dropping instead of huffing. Pro move: bring coffee from the resort.
🟢 Easy • 0.4 mi 📍 8,100 ft elevation 🌅 Arrive by 5:30 AM for best color
Bryce Canyon's hoodoos at first light — pink and orange and completely surreal.
🥶 Dress Warm July mornings at 8,100 feet can be 45–55°F. Bring a layer you can peel off once the sun rises. Cold + spectacular = worth every shiver.
12:00 PM
🤠 Lunch
Rustler's Restaurant
No Reservation Needed
📋 View Menu
A rustic Utah lunch spot with all the Western charm — generous portions, good burgers, and the honest-cooking energy of a place where people actually work the land. Perfect for refueling between hikes.
👕 Attire

Post-hike casual is the entire aesthetic here. Hiking pants, trail shoes, and a hat are standard dress code. Nobody's changing out of their gear — and nobody expects you to.

1:30 PM
🥾 Hike
Navajo Loop Trail — The Best Hike in Bryce
If you hike one trail in Bryce Canyon, make it this one. The Navajo Loop plunges you into the amphitheater — past Thor's Hammer (a balanced rock on an absurdly narrow column that defies physics), through Wall Street (a slot canyon with towering fir trees growing improbably from its floor), and around some of the most dramatic hoodoo formations on the planet. The descent is steep and exciting; the climb back out is short but rewarding. Bring water. Bring your camera. Bring snacks. You'll stop every 30 seconds.
🟠 Moderate • 1.3–1.5 mi ⬇️ 550 ft elevation change ⏱️ 1–2 hours
⚠️ 2026 Trail Note The Wall Street section is closed Mon–Thu, 6 AM–Noon for trail work in 2026. You're hiking Friday — you're all clear! The Two Bridges alternate is always open as a backup regardless.
4:00 PM
🌲 Hike
Bristlecone Loop Trail — Walk Among Ancients
A complete change of pace — and totally worth it. At Rainbow Point (the far south end of the park, 9,100 feet elevation), this gentle 1-mile loop winds through a subalpine forest past trees that were saplings during the Roman Empire. The oldest Bristlecone Pine here is over 1,800 years old. Stand next to it and try not to feel tiny. Views stretch to the Four Corners region. Blissfully uncrowded compared to the main amphitheater.
🟢 Easy • 1.0 mi loop 📍 9,100+ ft elevation 🌲 1,800-year-old trees
The trees have been alive since before the Vikings. Let that sink in.
7:00 PM
✨ Dinner
Sky Nova Bar & Grill — Dinner Under Glass
Reservations Made
📋 View Menu
This is not your average resort restaurant. Sky Nova is a celestial-themed, futuristic dining room inside a massive glass dome — 28 feet of floor-to-ceiling panoramic glass overlooking the canyon while you eat. Vegan and gluten-free options, cocktails (budget tip: stick to beer and wine — cocktails at Utah resorts are notoriously pricey), and an atmosphere that feels genuinely otherworldly. It's the resort's crown jewel and the perfect reward for Day 2.
👕 Attire

Smart casual — this is the nicest dinner of the trip so far. No need to dress formally, but swap trail shoes for cleaner footwear if you have it. Clean jeans, a nice top or button-down, and you're golden. The dome does the heavy lifting on atmosphere anyway.

🚗 Drive to Zion
Scenic Drive to East Zion (~1.5 hrs)
The drive from Bryce Canyon to East Zion on Hwy 89 is one of the most stunning road corridors in America — red rock formations, desert valleys, and the dramatic approach of Zion's sandstone towers. Blast a playlist and soak it in.
10:30 AM
🧗 Adventure — ALREADY BOOKED
Elkheart Canyon Guided Tour + UTV Tour
✅ Booked & Confirmed
Here it is. The big one. This is the activity you'll be talking about for years. East Zion Adventures takes you by UTV through the rugged backcountry mountains — then you walk through various slot canyons, with the talles wall reaching 100 feet straight up.
🎯 100 ft walls ⏱️ 2–3 hours 👶 Ages 8+ • No experience needed
🩱 Wear & Bring Closed-toe shoes with good grip — recommended. Clothes you don't mind getting dusty or slightly wet (the canyon can have water). Sunscreen, water, and please attach your phone/camera with a strap. The guides are excellent photographers for your triumphant moments.
Elkheart Canyon — private backcountry access, walls soaring 100+ feet, certified guides, zero regrets.

🌮 Lunch
Thunderbird Café

📋 View Menu

You're fresh off a canyoneering adventure — dusty, exhilarated, completely comfortable in whatever you rappelled in. Super casual here. Hiking clothes are 100% the norm.

2:00 PM
🐉 Hike
Belly of the Dragon — The Coolest Weird Photo Stop in Utah
In a state full of spectacular slot canyons, Belly of the Dragon is the fun, strange, Instagram-perfect wildcard. Originally a man-made drainage tunnel under Hwy 89, years of water flow carved the walls into rippled, rib-like patterns that look exactly like the inside of a dragon. It's dark, atmospheric, and genuinely eerie. There's a 4–5 foot scramble to get in (half the fun). Beyond the tunnel: sandy wash with canyon walls, perfect for photos and an easy stroll. Quirky, free, and under an hour — the ideal adventure between bigger adventures.
🟢 Easy • 1.8 mi round trip 🔦 Bring a flashlight! ⛈️ Do NOT enter if raining — flash flood risk
The Belly of the Dragon — weird, wonderful, and completely free. Perfect between the big adventures.
3:30 PM
🥾 Hike
Canyon Overlook Trail — Zion's Best Bang for Your Buck
Literally nicknamed "the best bang for the buck in Zion" by hiking guides everywhere, and deservedly so. This 1-mile round trip packs in catwalks above a sinuous slot canyon, shaded grottos, slickrock scrambling, and delivers a jaw-dropping panorama: West Temple, Towers of the Virgins, the Streaked Wall, the massive Great Arch below you. The East Temple towers 2,200 feet directly above. Note: no shuttle stop here — private vehicle only, parking is limited and fills early.
🟠 Moderate • 1.0 mi round trip ⏱️ ~1 hour 🅿️ Limited parking — arrive early or expect overflow
One mile of effort. A lifetime of memory. Canyon Overlook, Zion National Park.
6:30 PM
🍔 Dinner
Cowboy's Smokehouse or The Pizza Place
No Reservation Needed
📋 Cowboy's Menu 📋 Pizza Place Menu
Two great options depending on how the group is feeling. Or if everyone's wiped out and just wants to decompress, grab a pizza from The Pizza Place in Tropic and take it back to the resort — eat under the stars in your glass dome with zero effort required. Mossy Cave Trail
A short, easy stroll through hoodoos along a creek — ending at a split where you can visit a shaded moss-covered grotto AND a small waterfall, both within a few hundred yards. A perfect family-friendly morning that delivers genuine Bryce Canyon magic without breaking a sweat. At under a mile round trip, it's basically all payoff and no suffering.

Mossy Cave Trail — Short hike with plenty of payoff
3:00 PM
💦 Hike
Willis Creek Narrows Trail — As much, or as little as we want
A free, no-permit slot canyon where you simply walk in and let the walls close in around you — 200-foot sandstone narrows, a small waterfall, and a creek to hop across the whole way. The best part: the most stunning sections are in the first 1.3 miles, so go as far as the group feels like and turn around whenever you're ready. No pressure, all payoff.
🟠 Moderate • 6.1 mi round trip (for full hike) ⏱️ 2–3 hours, or whatever we want it to be 💦 slot canyon walls and water
Willis Creek Narrows Trail — Final Slot Canyons
6:30 PM
🏕️ Dinner
Bryce Canyon Lodge Restaurant — The Grand Finale
No Reservation — Arrive When They Open (5 PM) to Beat the Wait
📋 View Menu
Save your best meal for this one. The Lodge at Bryce Canyon Restaurant is a National Historic Landmark — a grand dining room with a soaring stone fireplace, rich wood beams, and towering pines outside the windows. The menu features locally-influenced, sustainably sourced dishes: buffalo sirloin steak, pineapple-glazed pork tenderloin, almond-panko-crusted trout, elk chili. The atmosphere is unmistakably western-elegant and deeply memorable. Arrive at 5 PM when they open to get good seats — by 6:30 PM it'll be packed.
👕 Attire — Step It Up Tonight

"National Park dressy" — clean jeans or chinos, a nicer top or button-down shirt, real shoes instead of sandals or trail runners. You don't need a blazer, but this is your one fancy meal of the trip. Think: classy enough to feel special, comfortable enough to still feel like you're on vacation.

🍷 Serves wine & beer 🌿 Sustainable + organic ingredients 🏛️ National Historic Landmark
8:30 PM
🌇 Evening Walk
Sunset Point — Your Victory Lap
Walk it off and soak it all in. Sunset Point gives you the Bryce Amphitheater glowing in late golden light — hoodoos turning from pink to amber to deep red as the sun sets behind you. The easy paved rim walk between Sunset and Sunrise Point is accessible and magical at this hour. You've hiked this canyon, rappelled into slot canyons, walked among ancient trees, and swum under a desert waterfall. This is your victory lap, and it's a beautiful one.
📸 Golden Hour Alert July sunset at Bryce Canyon is around 8:45–9:00 PM. The light from 7:30–9:00 PM is extraordinary — long shadows, warm amber tones, far fewer people than midday. Charge your phone. You will take one hundred photos.

Don't You Dare Forget
The Essential Packing List
☀️
Sun Protection

SPF 50+ sunscreen, wide-brim hat, UV sunglasses. Utah at altitude is absolutely relentless.

🥾
Hiking Shoes

Closed-toe with grip. Mandatory for Elkheart Canyon. Good tread saves your knees on the Navajo Loop descent.

💧
Hydration

Reusable water bottles. 2L+ per person per hike minimum. The desert will humble even experienced hikers.

🩱
Swimsuit

Essential for Lower Calf Creek Falls. The swimming hole is incredible and you'll regret not having one.

🔦
Flashlight / Headlamp

Belly of the Dragon tunnel + early morning hikes. Phone flashlight works in a pinch but bring a real one.

🧥
Layers

Bryce Canyon mornings at 8,000 ft can be 45°F. A lightweight fleece or jacket goes a very long way.
